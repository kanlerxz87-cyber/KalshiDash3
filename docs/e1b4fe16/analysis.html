Fees (formula from the published schedule)

General taker fee: round up(0.07 × C × P × (1 − P)), where P is the contract price in dollars and C the number of contracts. Maker fee: round up(0.0175 × C × P × (1 − P)). There is no settlement fee.

Worked example, computed before rounding: C = 100 contracts at P = $0.46 gives 0.07 × 100 × 0.46 × 0.54 = $1.7388 (general fee). The schedule rounds up to the centicent, so the exact charge needs the schedule's rounding rule. Source: Kalshi fee schedule (page 1 of 2). Per-series multipliers are not fully confirmed (flag D16).
