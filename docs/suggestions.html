The decision that unlocks the most

G5 (fees) is the highest-value open decision. Every performance number on this site is currently gross of per-series fee differences. At a 50¢ entry the general taker fee is about 2¢ per contract — roughly 4% of the stake — so a strategy that looks break-even gross is a loser net. Until fees are settled, treat any P&L comparison as a ranking of ideas, not of outcomes. Source: Kalshi fee schedule.
