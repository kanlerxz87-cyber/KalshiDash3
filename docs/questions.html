Open questions — what we know, what we don't, how to find out

Q1. What is the official definition of "highest dollar amount"?

Known: API returns volume_fp, open_interest_fp, last_price_dollars separately (Get Markets). Unknown: which field best represents "highest dollar amount" for your decision. Current choice: notional ≈ volume × last price (labeled estimate). To discover: compare rankings by volume vs. open interest vs. notional on live data; pick one and record it here.

Q2. Which series belong to each of the 5 categories?

Known: series have ticker/title/category (Get Series List). Unknown: exact Gas/Elections/Culture/Crypto/Golf series tickers — discovery is keyword-based and may miss or misclassify. To discover: review the matched-series list on the Verification page; approve/reject each match (Suggestion G6).

Q3. Where are the demo API keys for the real portfolio?

Known: keys are asymmetric pairs; private key never leaves your system; demo/prod keys differ (API Keys). Unknown: no keys were provided. To discover: create keys at demo.kalshi.co → Account Settings → API Keys, then set env vars for scripts/kalshi_client.py. Never paste keys into chat or commit them.

Q4. Should trading be paper-only or live on demo?

Known: demo uses mock funds and may not reflect real markets (Demo Environment). Unknown: your risk appetite and position-size limits. To discover: paper-trade first (Portfolio page), then decide max contracts per trade and per category.

Q5. Which chart window matters most (seconds / minutes / hours)?

Known: candlesticks support 1m/60m/1440m periods (Get Market Candlesticks); live polling covers seconds. Unknown: which horizon drives your decisions. To discover: use the window switcher on the dashboard; log which window you check before each paper trade.

Q6. What are the fee and rate-limit constraints for our polling cadence?

Known: default cost 10 tokens/request; Read/Write budgets by tier; 429 on exceed (Rate Limits). Unknown: our account tier. To discover: call GET /account/limits once keys exist; tune poll interval to stay under budget.
