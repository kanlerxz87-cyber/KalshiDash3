Trade categorization & strategies

Every trade is auto-categorized (Gas / Elections / Culture / Crypto / Golf / Other) by ticker keywords — the same mapping used on the dashboard. Strategies below are hypotheses, not proven edges. Each states its evidence requirement and risk. Nothing here is financial advice.

How to validate

1) Execute paper trades on the Portfolio page. 2) Watch the category P&L table. 3) Only promote a strategy to "supported" after ≥30 categorized trades with logged fills. 4) Re-verify against settled results (yes/no) on the dashboard's resolution tracker. Settlement mechanics: Market Settlement.
