2 · What “highest dollar amount” can honestly mean

Kalshi exposes several different size quantities, and they are not interchangeable:

FieldOfficial meaningUsable as a “biggest market” ranking?
volume_fpContracts traded on the marketYes — a count, so it ignores price
volume_24h_fpContracts traded in the last 24 hoursYes, and it is the better measure of “live right now”
open_interest_fpContracts held, not nettedYes — measures exposure rather than activity
notional_value_dollars“The total value of a single contract at settlement in dollars”No. It is a per-contract settlement value, not a traded volume

Sources: Get Markets. This project shows the labelled estimate volume_fp × last_price_dollars and, separately, raw contracts and open interest, so you can re-rank on the axis you actually care about.

3 · The four things a decision-maker should know

Demo ≠ real

Kalshi states demo prices “may not be reflective of those in real markets”. Every ranking here is a demo ranking. Source

Anonymous REST polling works; WebSocket still needs a key

The site polls public REST endpoints from the browser (subject to CORS and undocumented unauthenticated limits), while every WebSocket session remains key-authenticated — so tick streaming is still blocked on credentials. Source · Rate limits

Fees decide marginal strategies

The general taker fee is 0.07 × C × P × (1−P), about 2¢ per contract at 50¢ — roughly 4% of stake. The auto-sim charges this fee on every paper fill, so its P&L is net of the general rate (per-series multipliers still vary). Source

One step needs you

A demo account and API key must be created by a person. That is the only blocker to real demo performance reporting and to placing the auto-sim's trades for real. Until then everything trades in labelled simulation. Source

4 · How the numbers stay honest

A scheduled, credential-free collector reads public demo endpoints and commits a timestamped snapshot. A validator refuses a snapshot that invents a price, coerces a result to yes/no, or lacks provenance — and now also refuses any committed alert whose delta is smaller than the required $0.10, in either direction. On top of the snapshot layer, the browser can poll the same public endpoints directly (with a CORS self-test and automatic fallback), and the auto-sim ledger records every fill's price basis so the simulation can be audited. The pipeline is described on the home page, the rules on Verification, and everything that failed or is unresolved on Flagged for Review.
