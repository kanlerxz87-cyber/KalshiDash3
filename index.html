How to use

  1. Dashboard — live ranking, price graph (seconds/minutes/hours), liquidity ladder, track-until-resolved.
  2. Category pages — Gas · Elections · Culture · Crypto · Golf.
  3. Resolved — markets that finished yes/no.
  4. Portfolio — connect your own demo key (browser-only) to see balance/positions/fills, analyze trades, and place demo orders.
  5. Suggestions — improvement proposals + open questions. Sources — every claim linked; unverified items flagged.
