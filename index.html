Kalshi demo dashboard — highest-dollar live events in five categories

Tracks open Kalshi demo markets in Gas Prices, Elections, Culture, Crypto and Golf only, ranks them by an explicitly labelled dollars-traded estimate, follows each market until it resolves, charts contract price over time with the contracts available at each price, and analyses a clearly-labelled paper portfolio. Every live number comes from a committed snapshot of the official demo API, so it can be audited and re-run. Nothing here is hand-typed.
