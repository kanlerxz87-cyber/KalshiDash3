Auto-sim trading engine AUTO-SIM · PAPER ONLY

Automated simulated trades that test strategies against live demo-API prices. The engine runs by itself: it watches the same ≥$0.10 swing alerts as the alert chats, opens and closes paper positions, settles them from the API's literal result field, and categorises every trade by strategy, topic and price band. It places no real orders — demo order placement needs API keys that do not exist yet (F-07, F-27). The ledger lives in this browser (localStorage) and can be exported.

What "simulated" means here, precisely. Entry fills are simulated at the best quoted ask for the side bought (YES ask, or the NO ask derived from the YES bid per the documented reciprocal order-book relationship — Order Book Responses). Exits fill at the YES bid when quoted, otherwise the last traded price; exits that would need an unquoted price are not taken. Fees are modelled at the documented general taker rate ceil(M×0.07×C×P×(1−P)) (fee schedule); per-series fee multipliers differ, so net P&L is an estimate. Settlement pays $1 per winning contract because the official docs state YES outcomes pay YES holders and NO outcomes pay NO holders, with zero settlement fees for simple yes/no (Market Settlement). Markets whose result is scalar are left open and flagged rather than guessed.
