Live desk — swings ≥ 10¢, ranked by modelled ROI after fees

This page talks to the public Kalshi demo REST API straight from your browser, so the feeds move on a poll cadence (about every 5 seconds) instead of waiting for the scheduled snapshot, whose cadence GitHub treats as best-effort. If your browser cannot reach the API, it falls back to the committed snapshot and says so in the status bar — it never silently pretends to be live. Only the five requested topics are scanned, and only markets that are still open are charted until they resolve.

Order-book depth — contracts available at each price

From GET /markets/orderbooks, which returns bids only, ascending, best last, and needs no authentication; a NO bid at $Y is a YES ask at $1−Y, so the right column is what a YES buyer can actually lift (order-book responses). Empty arrays mean an empty book, not a missing value (documented). tickers is sent as a repeated parameter (?tickers=A&tickers=B), which is what this endpoint declares — 1–100 of them, 1–100 per request — and not as a comma-separated list the way GET /markets wants: a comma-joined list is treated as one unrecognised ticker and answered with empty arrays, which is indistinguishable from an empty book. "Book coverage N of M requested" below says which of the two happened.

Watchlist — largest live markets in the five topics, scored

Ranked by the labelled dollars-traded estimate (volume × last price, computed here — Kalshi has no “dollars traded” field). ROI is the modelled return on capital after the documented taker fee; a market that does not pass the screen shows the gate that stopped it instead of a number. Prices update on the poll cadence shown in the status bar.

Resolution tracker

Markets are followed until they resolve; the result column is the literal result field (yes, no, scalar or empty) — nothing is coerced into yes/no (settlement). Settled markets and their candles move to GET /historical/… once the cutoff advances, so the cutoff this page last read is printed in the status bar (historical data).

What this page is not. Demo environment, mock funds; Kalshi states demo prices “may not be reflective of those in real markets” (demo environment). The ROI figure is a modelled edge from the current book, not a forecast, and it is measured out-of-sample here — with the limits of that measurement stated next to it. Nothing on this page is a recommendation, and no order is ever placed by it.
