Trade Categorization & Strategies

Trades executed on Kalshi demo events are categorized by type, and strategies are created based on the type of trade. These are suggestion frameworks only — they are not market data and must not be treated as verified Kalshi values.

Trade type (category) Description Suggested strategy approach Applies to categories Status
Directional — Yes Buy yes contracts expecting the event to resolve Yes. Enter when yes bid shows depth (many contracts available at bid); set a target exit price and a stop based on event resolution timeline. Elections, Gas Prices, Crypto, Golf Suggestion — not implemented
Directional — No Buy no contracts expecting the event to resolve No. Mirror of Directional — Yes; size position by liquidity available at the no side bid/ask. Elections, Gas Prices, Crypto, Golf Suggestion — not implemented
Scalping / short-term Enter and exit within seconds–minutes as the contract price moves. Use the price graph with the seconds/minutes window; capture small bid–ask spreads where liquidity is deepest. Gas Prices, Crypto Suggestion — not implemented
Swing / hours Hold contracts across the hours window until news or time decay moves the price. Use the hours graph length; monitor resolution date proximity. Elections, Culture, Golf Suggestion — not implemented
Liquidity-based Trade where contracts are available at a given price (bid/ask size). Prefer markets with high open interest and tight bid–ask spreads; avoid thin markets with large slippage. All categories Suggestion — not implemented
Resolution trading Hold until the event resolves Yes or No; P&L determined by resolution. Track each event until resolved; record resolution in the portfolio trade log. All categories Suggestion — not implemented
How to use. Categorize each demo-account trade in the portfolio trade log by trade type, then review results by category in the Suggestions section before changing any strategy.
