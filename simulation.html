Paper strategies on live swing alerts

Four fixed rules are applied to every $0.10 swing in the alert archive (data/live/alerts_archive.json, built by the alert pipeline). Each fill is a paper position: no order is sent and no account is used. Results show what the rule would have produced at the prices that were actually stored, with the official taker fee deducted. Positions are grouped by topic and by trade type so each rule can be judged on the kind of trade it takes.

Paper only. A strategy is not treated as profitable or unprofitable until it has 30 settled positions. Demo prices may not reflect real markets (Demo Environment).

Why positions are missing

A signal fills only when a bid and ask were stored at the alert time. The collector stores quotes only while a market is in its open scan, so markets that have closed have none. Nothing is filled from last-trade prices.

Strategy results

Results by topic

Topics are kept separate. Counts are small, so read them as a record, not a verdict.

Results by trade type

Trade type = topic | direction of the move that triggered the signal | price band of the entry (below 0.20, 0.20 to 0.80, above 0.80).

Open positions (marked to the current bid)

Unrealised = current bid − entry − entry fee − exit fee at that bid. Blank when the market is not in the latest snapshot.

Settled and voided positions

VOID = the entry quote breached the spread rule (see Rules). Voided positions are not counted in results.
